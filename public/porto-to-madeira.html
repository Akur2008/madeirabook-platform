Frequently asked

When should I book my flight?

6+ months ahead. Prices triple in the final two weeks before departure. A €17 ticket in October can cost €150+ if booked last-minute.

What's the cheapest month to fly?

October and November — from €17 and €18, with six weeks below €25. Even Christmas is mild: peak is €167 vs €400+ on other European routes.

How long is the flight?

2 hours 5 minutes, direct. The shortest route from mainland Europe to Madeira.

Which airlines fly direct?

Ryanair (year-round, cheapest), easyJet, and TAP Air Portugal + Azores Airlines (year-round).

How do I get from Funchal airport to my apartment?

Taxi to central Funchal: ~€25, 20 min. Uber works. Public Aerobus: €5, 40 min.
