Where to Stay in Madeira

Madeira has 7 main areas, each with a different vibe. Below — real prices, pros and cons, and who each area suits. All apartments are booked directly from local hosts, no platform fees.

Quick answer: First time? Lido (Funchal). Family? Caniço de Baixo. Winter sun? Calheta. Peaceful? Ponta do Sol.

The 7 Best Areas to Stay in Madeira

1. Lido — Funchal

€80–150

The tourist heart of Funchal. Ocean promenade, sea pools, restaurants every 50 meters. Walkable. 20 min from airport.

Best for: First-time visitors, couples, anyone without a car.
Watch out: Busy, more expensive.

2. Funchal Old Town (Zona Velha)

€70–130

Historic cobblestone streets, painted doors, best restaurants in Madeira. Livelier at night.

Best for: Foodies, culture lovers, couples.
Watch out: Noisy at night, no ocean view.

3. Caniço de Baixo

€65–120

Quiet coastal village, 10 min east of Funchal. Ocean-view apartments, calm water, family-friendly.

Best for: Families, longer stays, quiet holidays.
Watch out: Need a car or buses.

4. Calheta

€70–130

Sunniest spot on the island. Man-made sandy beach, marina, mild winters. 30 min from Funchal.

Best for: Winter sun, families, beach lovers.
Watch out: Far from airport (45 min).

5. Ponta do Sol

€75–140

Small, charming village on the sunny south coast. Pebble beach, excellent restaurants, calm atmosphere.

Best for: Couples, digital nomads, romantic stays.
Watch out: Limited parking.

6. São Vicente

€60–100

North coast, green and dramatic. Lava pools, cliffs, fewer tourists. Cooler and wetter than south.

Best for: Hikers, nature lovers, solitude.
Watch out: Cooler weather, need a car.

7. Porto Moniz

€65–110

Famous volcanic lava pools on the northwest tip. Spectacular scenery. Quiet evenings.

Best for: Unique experiences, photography, escaping crowds.
Watch out: 1 hour from Funchal.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best area to stay in Madeira?

For first-time visitors, Lido (Funchal) is the best balance: ocean promenade, restaurants, 20 min to airport. For families, Caniço de Baixo. For winter sun, Calheta.

Is Funchal a good place to stay?

Yes. Funchal is the capital with the most restaurants, shops, and transport links. The Lido zone is the most popular for tourists — ocean views and walkable promenade.

Where is the warmest part of Madeira?

The southwest coast — Calheta, Ponta do Sol, and Paul do Mar — is the sunniest and warmest, especially in winter. Southwest gets 20–30% more sun than Funchal.

Should I stay in Funchal or Caniço?

Funchal (Lido) if you want walkable restaurants, nightlife, and tour pickups. Caniço de Baixo if you want quiet ocean views and prefer to drive everywhere.
