MADEIRA LOCAL GUIDE

Where to Eat in Madeira

Funchal has three Michelin-starred restaurants. Plus hundreds of local tabernas with fresh fish and ocean views.

Funchal highlights

Il Gallo dOro (2 Michelin stars)

At The Cliff Bay Hotel. Tasting menu from 195 EUR. The best restaurant on the island.

William (1 Michelin star)

At Reids Palace. Art Deco room, sea views, chef Luis Pestana.

Avista

Michelin-recommended, cliff overlooking ocean. Contemporary food, unforgettable views.

Casal da Penha

Top-rated on TheFork. Traditional Madeiran food, generous portions.

FAQ

Michelin restaurants? Three, all in Funchal: Il Gallo dOro, William, Avista.

Best local restaurant? Casal da Penha. For seafood, Old Town has dozens of options.
