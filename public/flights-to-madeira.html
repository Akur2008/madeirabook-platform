Frequently asked

What's the cheapest way to fly to Madeira?

Dublin with Ryanair — from €15 one-way, 3h 45min. Then Porto and Lisbon (both from €17). The UK is next — Liverpool and Manchester from €18.

Which cities have direct flights to Madeira?

Direct year-round: London (STN/LTN/LGW), Manchester, Liverpool, Edinburgh, Dublin, Paris (CDG), Porto, Lisbon, Frankfurt, Munich, Amsterdam, Madrid, Barcelona (summer), Brussels (Charleroi), Zurich, Vienna. Every other European city connects via Lisbon.

When should I book flights to Madeira?

6+ months ahead. Prices triple in the final two weeks. Around Christmas (Dec 20–28), even short-haul tickets jump 5–10x — book January instead.

Is it cheaper to fly direct or via Lisbon?

Depends on the city. From Amsterdam, Madrid, Barcelona, Berlin, Brussels, Zurich or Vienna — via Lisbon with TAP is significantly cheaper. From London, Manchester, Liverpool, Edinburgh, Dublin, Paris, Porto or Lisbon itself — direct is best.
