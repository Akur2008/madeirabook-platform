Frequently Asked Questions

How much does an apartment in Madeira cost per night?

Ocean-view apartments in Funchal start from €70–90 per night in low season, €100–150 in summer. Studios from €55. Villas from €150. Direct booking saves 10–15% compared to Airbnb.

Is it cheaper to book direct in Madeira?

Yes. On Madeirabook you book directly from the local host — no platform commission, no guest service fees. Same apartment, 10–15% cheaper than Booking or Airbnb.

What is included in a Madeira apartment rental?

Most Madeirabook apartments include wifi, kitchen, washing machine, and often a sea-view balcony. Linens, towels, and final cleaning are included. Free cancellation up to 24 hours.

When is the cheapest time to rent in Madeira?

November and early December are cheapest (up to 40% less than summer). Winter sun in January–March is the next best value — +22°C, low prices, quiet beaches.
