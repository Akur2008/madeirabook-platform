Best months to visit Madeira

🥇 April–June

Warm (+22–24°C), not crowded yet, Flower Festival in April–May. Best balance of weather and price.

🥇 September–October

Still warm (+24–26°C), fewer tourists than summer, cheaper flights. Wine Week in September.

💰 Cheapest: November

Averages €50 one-way. Quieter, some rain, but still +22°C. Best value.

❌ Avoid: August

Peak season — flights triple, hotels full, beaches packed. Same island, worse value.

FAQ

What is the best month to visit Madeira?

April–June and September–October. Warm, not crowded, best value.

Is Madeira warm in winter?

Yes — +20–22°C in January. Warmest destination in Europe in winter.

What is the cheapest time to visit?

November and early December. Flights from €50 one-way.

Can you swim in Madeira in winter?

Yes. Water stays +19–20°C all year. South coast is warmest.
