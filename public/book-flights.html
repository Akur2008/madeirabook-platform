Quick answers

How do I search for flights to Madeira here?

Enter your origin city and dates in the search widget — results come from Aviasales in real time. Madeira's airport code is FNC (Funchal).

What are the cheapest routes to Madeira?

Dublin from €15, Porto from €17, Lisbon from €17, Liverpool from €18 and Manchester from €18 — one-way, direct where available.

When should I book flights to Madeira?

6+ months ahead. Real Google Flights data shows the same seat costs €27 six months out and €280 two weeks before departure.

Which airport serves Madeira?

Cristiano Ronaldo International Airport (FNC), near Funchal. All direct flights from Europe land there.
