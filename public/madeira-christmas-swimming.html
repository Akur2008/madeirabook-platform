MADEIRA · WINTER SUN

Christmas in Madeira — Swim While Europe Freezes

December in Europe means −5°C, grey skies, and heavy coats. December in Madeira means +22°C, ocean swims, and Christmas lights that fill the entire capital.

Quick answer: Yes, you can swim in December. Water is +19°C, air is +22°C. Funchal lights run Nov–Jan. Flights from €15. Book 6+ months ahead for best prices.

Where to Swim in December

🌊 Calheta Beach

Man-made sandy beach on the south coast. Warmest water, calm waves, sunniest spot on the island.

→ Best for: families, winter swimming

♨️ Porto Moniz Lava Pools

Natural volcanic pools on the north-west tip. Protected from waves. Unforgettable experience.

→ Best for: unique swim, photography

🏊 Lido Complex (Funchal)

Sea-water pools and direct ocean access. Heated options. Promenade, restaurants, easy to reach.

→ Best for: convenience, no car needed

🏝 Ponta do Sol

Pebble beach, dark sand, warm water. Small, quiet, and local. Great restaurants nearby.

→ Best for: couples, calm atmosphere

Christmas in Funchal — What to Expect

🎄 Christmas lights (late Nov – early Jan): Funchal turns into a light show. Every street, every square, every garden is decorated. Free to walk.

🎆 New Year's Eve fireworks: One of the largest fireworks displays in the world — a Guinness World Record. Ships come from all over Europe to watch it from the sea.

🍷 Poncha and bolo do caco: Traditional warm drinks and street food fill the old town. Locals and tourists mix in the streets.

☀️ Beach days between Christmas and New Year: While the rest of Europe shovels snow, you can swim, hike, and eat lunch outside.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you swim in the ocean in Madeira in December?

Yes. Atlantic water temperature in December is around +19°C — warm enough for most swimmers, especially on the south coast. Air temperature is +20–22°C.

What is the weather in Madeira at Christmas?

Christmas in Madeira is mild: +18–22°C during the day, +14–16°C at night. Funchal and the south coast are warmest. Some rain is possible but usually brief.

Are there Christmas lights in Funchal?

Yes — Funchal has one of the most famous Christmas light displays in Europe, running from late November to early January. New Year's fireworks are among the largest in the world.

Is January a good time to visit Madeira?

Yes. January is quiet, cheap, and still +20°C. Flights from Europe are often €15–40. Best month for winter sun without crowds.
