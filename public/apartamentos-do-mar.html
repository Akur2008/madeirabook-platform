Apartamentos do Mar — Lido, Funchal

Same building, same promenade, same ocean views as the 5-star hotels next door. But you pay for the night, not for the lobby.

Same location, different price

Apartamentos do Mar is on the first line of Funchal's Lido promenade — the same stretch where 5-star hotels charge 250+ EUR per night. The building itself is an ex-4-star hotel: solid construction, sea views, direct access to the promenade. Service is simpler, apartments are older, but the location is identical.

If you spend your days hiking the levadas and swimming in the ocean, you only need the apartment for sleeping. And for that, €100 makes more sense than €250.

What is around

10 km promenade

Funchal's Lido promenade runs along the ocean for about 10 km — from Lido to Praia Formosa. Walk, run, cycle, watch the sunrise.

5-star neighbours

The Cliff Bay, Reid's Palace, Belmond — all within 500m. Same beach, same promenade. You just sleep elsewhere.

Ocean pools and beaches

Complexo Balnear do Lido and Ponta Gorda — heated ocean-water pools, direct sea access, 200m away.

Restaurants

Within 300m: dozens of restaurants, from cheap fish taverns to 5-star dining. Many have TheFork deals 30-50% off.

FAQ

Why is it cheaper than the hotels next door?

Apartamentos do Mar is an ex-4-star hotel with simpler service and older apartments. No lobby, no pool of its own, no concierge. But the location is identical.

Is it safe?

Yes — same neighbourhood, same streets, same promenade. Lido is one of the safest parts of Funchal.

Do I need a car?

No. Everything within 5-15 min walking: restaurants, supermarkets, beaches, ocean pools. Public transport to Old Town and airport nearby.
