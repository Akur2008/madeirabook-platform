MADEIRA · WHEN TO GO

Madeira Events Calendar

The best months in Madeira aren't summer. Carnival, Flower Festival, Wine Week, Christmas — full calendar below.

Full Year Calendar

🎭 Carnival of Madeira

February–March

One of the biggest carnivals in Europe. Parades, costumes, music, all-night celebration in Funchal.

🌸 Flower Festival

April–May

Funchal covered in flowers. Parade, flower carpets in the streets, wall of hope at Praça do Município.

🎆 Atlantic Festival

June

Saturday fireworks shows over the bay of Funchal. Free. One of the best fireworks festivals in Portugal.

🏁 Madeira Rally (WRC)

July–August

World Rally Championship stage on Madeira's mountain roads. Night stages, spectacular views.

🍷 Wine Week (Festa do Vinho)

August–September

Wine tastings, grape treading, folk music. Held in Estreito de Câmara de Lobos and Funchal.

🎄 Christmas & New Year

December–January

Funchal lights (Nov–Jan), Europe's biggest fireworks on New Year's Eve — Guinness World Record. Swim in the ocean between Christmas and New Year.

Best events for your trip

🎨 For culture

Flower Festival (April–May) and Wine Week (Sept) are the most photogenic.

🎆 For spectacle

Carnival (Feb) and New Year's Eve fireworks (Dec 31) are the biggest shows.

FAQ

What is the biggest event in Madeira?

New Year's Eve fireworks — one of the largest in the world, Guinness World Record.

When is Carnival in Madeira?

February–March, dates change yearly. Usually 40 days before Easter.

What is the Flower Festival?

April–May. Funchal covered in flowers, with a parade and flower carpets in the streets.

Are events free?

Most parades and fireworks are free. Wine tastings and some events require tickets.
