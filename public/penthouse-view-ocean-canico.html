About this penthouse

Set on the sunny south coast of Madeira in Caniço, this top-floor apartment offers unobstructed views over the Atlantic. Three bedrooms and five beds make it ideal for families or groups of friends travelling together.

The living room opens onto a private balcony where you can watch the sunrise over the ocean. A fully equipped kitchen, air conditioning, and fast Wi-Fi cover the essentials. The building is quiet, and parking is available on the street.

Caniço is known for its mild climate and local restaurants. The nearest beach (Reis Magos) is a 5-minute drive, and Funchal is 15 minutes by car.
