FUNCHAL · LIDO · LOCAL GUIDE

Lido, Funchal — the Ocean Promenade

10 km of ocean-front promenade, heated pools with sea water, dozens of restaurants, and the best morning walk in Madeira.

Why Lido

Lido is the modern, ocean-facing part of Funchal — the strip between the Old Town and Praia Formosa. It's where the 5-star hotels are, where the promenade runs along the sea, and where most visitors end up staying.

What's different from the Old Town: no cobblestones, wider streets, calmer, quieter at night. What's the same: still 15 min from downtown Funchal, still walking distance to beaches and restaurants.

The promenade

A continuous 10 km ocean walk — from the Lido complex west to Praia Formosa and the harbour of Camara de Lobos. Paved, flat, well-lit. Morning runners, evening walkers, sunset photographers.

Ocean pools and beaches

Complexo Balnear do Lido

Public ocean-water pools with direct sea access. Heated in winter. Entry €5-7. Sunbeds and umbrellas available.

Ponta Gorda

Small lido further west. Quiet, local, cheaper. Great for morning swims before the crowds.

Praia Formosa

Pebble beach, dark volcanic sand, ocean waves. About 2 km west along the promenade. Free access.

Doca do Cavacas

Natural ocean pool near Praia Formosa. Popular for snorkelling.

Where to stay in Lido

5-star hotels

Reid's Palace (since 1891), The Cliff Bay, Belmond Reid's, Pestana Grand. 250-600 EUR per night. The classic Lido experience.

4-star hotels

Pestana Promenade, Vila Gale, Melia Madeira Mare. 120-200 EUR per night.

Apartments (best value)

Ocean-view apartments in buildings like Apartamentos do Mar — same promenade, same views, but 80-130 EUR per night. Full kitchens, no service. See our apartments.

Where to eat

Lido has dozens of restaurants. Highlights: traditional fish taverns (espada, lapas), Italian (Il Basilico), sushi (Sushi d'Art), and the classic ocean-view dining at the hotels. Many have TheFork deals 30-50% off.

FAQ

Is Lido better than the Old Town?

Depends on you. Lido is calmer, cleaner, ocean-facing. Old Town is busier, more traditional, more restaurants. If you want to sleep well and walk by the sea — Lido. If you want nightlife — Old Town.

Do I need a car in Lido?

No. The promenade is walkable. Buses, taxis, and tour pickups all work from here.

Can I swim in the ocean in Lido?

Yes — ocean-water pools at the Lido complex, and direct sea access from Ponta Gorda and Doca do Cavacas. Water temperature stays 19-22°C year-round.
