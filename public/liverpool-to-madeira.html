Frequently asked

When should I book my flight?

6+ months ahead. Prices triple in the final two weeks before departure. An €18 ticket in October can cost €180+ if booked last-minute.

What's the cheapest month to fly?

October — from €18, with five weeks below €35. Also May is cheap (from €62). Avoid Christmas (Dec 20–28, up to €508) and August (from €145).

How long is the flight?

4 hours, direct. Jet2.com operates LPL–FNC, mostly in autumn and winter season.

Which airlines fly direct?

Jet2.com flies direct from Liverpool John Lennon. For year-round options, consider connecting via Manchester or Lisbon.

How do I get from Funchal airport to my apartment?

Taxi to central Funchal: ~€25, 20 min. Uber works. Public Aerobus: €5, 40 min.
